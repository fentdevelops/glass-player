Everything you already have.

Point Glassy at your files and you get a real library: albums, artists, folders, playlists and favorites, with search that forgives typos.

It stays fast with 10,000 songs. It doesn't come with any music, and your files stay where they are.

This is a working demo. Press anything. The songs are made up, and the music is made in your browser.

Lyrics that follow along.

Every line glides into place as it's sung. Click one to jump there.

Glassy looks for lyrics next to your song, inside its tags, then online. If it picks the wrong ones, search as you type and choose the right match.

Sound you can shape.

Drag the curve with your mouse. Add bass, add treble, put the song in a room, a plate or a hall.

Even out volume is on from the start, so quiet songs come up and loud ones settle down. Albums play straight through with no gap and no click.

Make it yours.

Tint the glass any colour. Put moving colours, the song's cover, or your own picture behind it. Try one.

Your messy folder gets better.

Edit titles, artists and albums, saved back into your MP3s. Find online fills them in for you.

Move, zoom, or borrow art from another song. See every song sharing a cover and change them all at once. Drag one song onto another and you've got an album.
