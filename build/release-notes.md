## Glass 0.2.8

**Simpler playback.** Settings → Playback is down to one thing: Fade in and out. Everything else just works one way now: clicking the song that's playing restarts it, Autoplay is always on, songs run straight into each other, and pause stops right away with no fade.

Installed copies update themselves. New install: download `Glass-Player-Setup-0.2.8.exe` below.
