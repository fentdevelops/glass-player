## Glassy 0.2.18

**A welcome for new installs.** The first time you open Glassy, a short intro shows you around, then glides into the app. People who already have a library don't see it. Settings can play it again.

**Here's what we found.** While the intro plays, Glassy looks through your Music and Downloads folders for songs and lists what it found, grouped and ready to tick. Short clips and game sounds are left unticked, and every row has a preview button. Not now leaves your library as it was. Settings → Library → Find songs on this PC opens the same list any time, without songs you already have.

**An optional account.** Sign up with an email or phone number, or with Google, and pick a username. The account stays on this computer, and Continue without an account skips it. Settings has an Account section to sign in, sign out or remove it.

**Also:** the playing song's cover is now the default background (moving colours until there's a picture), and a batch of small fixes.

Installed copies on Windows update themselves. New install:

- **Windows:** `Glassy-Setup-0.2.18.exe`
- **Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer):** `Glassy-0.2.18-mac-arm64.dmg`
- **Mac with an Intel chip:** `Glassy-0.2.18-mac-x64.dmg`

The Mac files appear here a few minutes after the Windows ones. Glassy isn't signed with an Apple developer account yet, so the first time you open it on a Mac, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. The [README](https://github.com/fentdevelops/glass-player#opening-it-on-a-mac-for-the-first-time) has the steps.
