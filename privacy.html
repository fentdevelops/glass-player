Privacy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Glassy runs on your computer. There's no Glassy server and nobody at Glassy can see your library or what you play.

What stays on your computer

  • Your music and videos
  • Your library, playlists, favorites, tags and settings

When Glassy goes online

Glassy contacts these services for these reasons, and it sends only what's listed.

WhyWho it talks toWhat it sends
Finding lyricsLRCLIB, Kugou, NetEaseSong title and artist, plus album and length when it has them
Filling in song details and covers, when you press Find onlineApple's iTunes search, DeezerWhat you searched for
Artist photos, bios and name suggestionsWikipedia, DeezerThe artist's name
Checking for updatesGitHubThe usual details of any web request

Like any web request, these reveal your computer's internet address to the service you're reaching. Their own privacy policies apply to them.

Glassy doesn't contain ads or analytics, and it doesn't sell anything about you.

This website

This site doesn't use cookies or analytics. It's hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may keep basic visitor logs. Looking up the latest version asks GitHub from your browser.

If you search for an artist in the player here, what you type goes to Apple's iTunes search, and the covers and 30-second previews come from Apple. Apple's privacy policy applies to that. To show lyrics, the song's title, artist and album go to LRCLIB. This site doesn't keep your searches.

Removing your information

Uninstalling Glassy doesn't delete your music. To clear everything else, delete Glassy's data folder.

Children

Glassy isn't aimed at children under 13.
