Not your OS?

Your download has started. One more step on a Mac.

Apple checks apps that are signed through its paid developer program. Glassy isn't in that program yet, so the first time you open it macOS says it can't check the app. Here's how to get past that once:

  1. Open the .dmg and drag Glassy into Applications.
  2. Open Glassy. macOS says it can't check the app. Click Done.
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down to the Glassy message, and click Open Anyway.

If macOS says the app is "damaged", paste this into Terminal and open it again:

xattr -cr /Applications/Glassy.app

Opening it on a Mac for the first time

Glassy isn't signed with an Apple developer account yet, so macOS stops it the first time. It takes a minute to get past.

  1. Open the .dmg and drag Glassy into Applications.
  2. Open Glassy. macOS says it can't check the app. Click Done.
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down to the Glassy message, and click Open Anyway.

If macOS says the app is "damaged", paste this into Terminal and open it again:

xattr -cr /Applications/Glassy.app

To update on a Mac, download the new version from the card Glassy shows you and drag it over the old one. Your library stays where it is.

Windows says it's unrecognised

The Windows files aren't signed either, so SmartScreen may warn you the first time. Click More info, then Run anyway.
