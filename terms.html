Terms

Glassy is a personal music player that lives on your computer. This is the whole agreement, and it is short.

What this is

A personal app that runs on your computer. There is no Glassy server.

Your music

Your music and videos stay where they are, and Glassy does not upload them.

When Glassy goes online

Only to look up lyrics, artist info and covers, and to check for updates.

What you play

Only use Glassy with files you have the right to play.

As is

Glassy is provided as is, with no promise that it will always work or be free of mistakes.

Copying

Glassy isn't open source. You're welcome to download and use it, but the code, design and name aren't free to copy.
